# Edo name and artwork

You may keep the Edo name for local development and testing, and use it to identify the upstream
project. Use a distinct name and artwork for distributed forks and hosted services. Avoid implying
that a fork is official or endorsed.

The [brand artwork terms](LICENSES/LicenseRef-Edo-Brand.txt) apply to artwork explicitly marked
with that license. These terms preserve rights allowed by applicable law and third-party licenses.
